## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the welcoming warmth, vibrancy, and practicality of modern Brazilian domestic culinary life. It balances functional efficiency (inventory tracking, pantry audits, expiration date alerts) with sensorial, appetizing delight (recipe curation, creative leftovers cooking, joyful domestic savings).

### Brand Pillars & Personality
- **Afetuoso & Acolhedor (Warm & Domestic):** Feels like stepping into a luminous Brazilian kitchen on a Saturday morning. Inviting, human, accessible, and completely unpretentious.
- **Consciente & Econômico (Resourceful & Mindful):** Celebrates the art of "aproveitamento integral" (zero waste cooking) and domestic budgeting without feeling clinical, restrictive, or punitive.
- **Vibrante & Fresco (Fresh & Appetizing):** Rich greens evoke leafy feira produce, herbs, and freshness; warm citrus oranges awaken appetite and drive decisive interaction.

### Visual Style
The interface builds on a localized interpretation of Material 3 and modern tactile surfaces:
- Soft off-white backdrops eliminate sterile digital glare and recreate the warmth of cotton kitchen linens and natural ceramics.
- Pure white elevated cards float on ultra-soft, warm-tinted shadows, giving tangible presence to ingredients and recipe cards.
- Rounded contours (16px to 24px) establish a soft, tactile, touch-friendly mobile landscape engineered for effortless one-handed use during food prep.

## Colors

The palette grounds the product in vitality, appetizing highlights, and clear legibility under bright kitchen lights.

### Role Allocation
- **Primary (`#2E7D32` - Folha Fresca):** Represents fresh ingredients, healthy choices, pantry stability, and core navigational anchors. Darker tone `#1B5E20` anchors text and high-contrast iconography; lighter tone `#4CAF50` serves as secondary badges, success tags, and freshness meters.
- **Secondary (`#FB8C00` / `#F57C00` - Tangerina Solar):** Reserved for high-intent culinary prompts, conversion CTAs (e.g., "Cozinhar Agora", "Aproveitar Ingredientes"), caloric indicators, and urgency accents (e.g., ingredients close to expiration).
- **Tertiary (`#8D6E63` - Especiarias da Terra):** Earthy warm spice tone for pantry categories (dry grains, beans, root vegetables, spices) and subtle decorative indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAFAF7` - Algodão Cru):** Soft warm off-white that prevents visual fatigue while enhancing the pure white `#FFFFFF` of content containers.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#263238` - Carvão Suave):** Deep slate neutral ensuring accessible contrast on light surfaces while maintaining softer optics than harsh true black.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - *Consumo Imediato (Urgent):* `#E53935` (Alerta de Vencimento)
  - *Aproveitamento / Atenção:* `#F57C00` (Vencimento Próximo)
  - *Fresco / No Prazo:* `#2E7D32` (Despensa Segura)

## Typography

Typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve an open, friendly, modern Brazilian culinary feel. Its wide apertures, soft geometric rounded terminals, and clear numerals guarantee legibility whether reading recipe measurements over a boiling pan or scanning food expiry stamps.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Section Titles (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Reserved for main greeting dashboards ("Olá, Mariana!", "O que vamos cozinhar hoje?") and card titles.
- **Content Blocks (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Engineered for preparation instructions, pantry descriptions, and step-by-step guides with comfortable 1.5x proportional line heights.
- **Tags, Numbers & Badges (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`):** Configured with medium-to-bold weights for unit measurements (`250g`, `3 un`), preparation times (`35 min`), and nutritional values (`320 kcal`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a mobile-first fluid container architecture calibrated for quick thumb reach and vertical thumb-scrolling ergonomics on iOS and Android devices.

### Grid & Margins
- **Mobile (<600px):** 4-column fluid layout with a base outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px) and column gutters of `1rem` (16px). This guarantees ample breathing room on edge-to-edge displays while maximizing touch targets.
- **Tablet / Large Mobile Foldables (600px-840px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins, shifting cards into multi-column masonry for pantry categorizations.

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
- Micro rhythm follows an 8pt base grid (`space-xs`: 4px, `space-sm`: 8px, `space-md`: 16px, `space-lg`: 24px, `space-xl`: 32px).
- Kitchen tasks require loose item separation to prevent accidental taps while moving quickly around the countertop. List items maintain a minimum height of 56px with `space-sm` inner vertical padding and `space-md` horizontal breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm ambient shadows with Material 3 tonal layering. Rather than cold gray dropshadows, shadows incorporate a subtle warm amber-tinted cast (`rgba(58, 45, 23, 0.06)` to `rgba(58, 45, 23, 0.12)`) that integrates naturally with the off-white `#FAFAF7` background.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Main background `#FAFAF7`.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` and ambient shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Used for pantry item rows, ingredient tiles, and settings groupings.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Recipe Showcases):** Elevated cards with `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`. Evokes tactile lift on tap or swipe.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Navigation & Modals):** Pure white container with top ambient diffusion `0 -4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Action Buttons & Alert Sheets):** Warm vibrant orange FAB with `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(251, 140, 0, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a rounded aesthetic (Level 2: base 8px, container 16px, interactive cards 20px, dialogs/sheets 24px) creating a friendly, organic, and kitchen-safe geometry.

### Radius Scale Applications
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** 12px to 16px corner radius for a soft, approachable feel.
- **Item Cards & Recipe Thumbnails:** 18px to 20px radius with clipped photographic assets.
- **Pills, Quantities & Status Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for quick glanceable status differentiation.
- **Bottom Navigation Surface:** Top corners rounded at 24px, docking seamlessly into phone gesture docks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Tangerina Solar (`#FB8C00` / `#F57C00`) with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text. Used for immediate culinary steps ("Adicionar à Despensa", "Cozinhar com Estes Ingredientes"). Pill-shaped or 16px radius, min-height 52px for optimal thumb targeting.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined or tinted green (`#E8F5E9` background, `#1B5E20` text). Used for auxiliary actions like filtering, bulk editing, or adjusting portions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background, `#2E7D32` bold text, dedicated to inline toggles and dismiss actions.

### Cards
- **Pantry Item Card:** Crisp white card (`#FFFFFF`) with 18px radius. Contains ingredient iconography, Brazilian unit measurements (`kg`, `g`, `un`, `maço`), days until expiry badge, and a quick-action decrementer.
- **Recipe Recommendation Card:** Media-first card with high-quality food photography top container, 150px-180px height, overlaid with prep time badge (`"25 min"`) and calories pill (`"340 kcal"` in `#FFF3E0` with `#E65100` text). Bottom container shows match percentage (e.g., `"Você tem 5 de 6 ingredientes"`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped tags (`"Todos"`, `"Vencendo Hoje"`, `"Freezer"`, `"Hortifrúti"`). Inactive state: white background with subtle border; active state: `#2E7D32` with white text.
- **Calories & Nutrition Badge:** Soft orange container (`#FFE0B2`) with bold `#E65100` label text.
- **Zero-Waste Alert Chip:** Soft red tint (`#FFEBEE`) with `#C62828` label for items requiring consumption within 24-48 hours.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Barra de Navegação)
- Fixed at screen bottom with 64px container height + safe area inset.
- High-elevation white surface with 24px top border radii.
- Four core destinations labeled in Brazilian Portuguese:
  1. **Despensa** (Pantry & Fridge inventory) — Icon: stylized pantry/fridge basket.
  2. **Cardápios** (Smart recipe suggestions & daily menus) — Icon: chef hat / plate with cutlery.
  3. **Favoritos** (Saved recipes & staple lists) — Icon: filled/outline heart.
  4. **Perfil** (Savings meter, household settings & preferences) — Icon: user avatar / house icon.
- Active state uses an pill highlight (`#E8F5E9`) with `#1B5E20` icon and bold typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Quick Add:** Light cream-tinted background (`#F1F1EB`), 16px radius, leading search icon, trailing barcode scanner shortcut icon.
- **Checkboxes & Quantity Steppers:** Rounded checkboxes (6px inner radius) in `#2E7D32`. Plus/minus quantity adjusters have a min tap target of 44x44px for effortless cooking-mode interaction.
